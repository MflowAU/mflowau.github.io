01. What guides us

[ Est. 2019 · Victoria ]

The decisions behind a piece of software shape the experience of using it. We believe those decisions deserve attention—from the purpose it serves to the expectations it creates.

Our work can take different forms. The principles behind it remain consistent.

  1. 01.1

    Clarity

    Make the important things understandable. Explain what is happening, what can be changed, and where the limits are.

  2. 01.2

    Respect

    People’s time, attention and information deserve consideration. Privacy and meaningful choice belong in the design conversation from the beginning.

  3. 01.3

    Care

    Look beyond the first impression. Consider the details, the difficult moments, and how the work holds up through everyday use.

02. How we build

[ 02.1 · 02.2 ]

Good work grows from a series of considered decisions. We aim to carry the same attention through the whole process: questioning, making, learning and improving.

02.1

Understand before adding

We begin by looking closely at the context: what people are trying to do, where the difficulty lies, and which assumptions deserve another look.

Curiosity helps us find a useful direction. Clear thinking helps us decide what belongs. Simplicity emerges from understanding what matters.

02.2

Build for the longer view

A first release is one point in the life of a piece of work.

We value foundations that can be understood, maintained and improved. We make room for feedback, changing circumstances and the lessons that only emerge through use.

The intention is to create work that continues to earn its place in people’s lives.
